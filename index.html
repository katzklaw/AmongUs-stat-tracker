Player

"Vote Accuracy" = of the votes you cast while on the crew team, how many were for someone who actually was an impostor - impostor-game votes aren't counted, since voting for your own teammate isn't a meaningful read. "Decisive Votes" = your vote's target was the one actually ejected (any role). "Task Completion" counts games where you finished 100% of tasks; "Exact Task Completion" is your true average % done, but only available for games logged in the newer format that records it. "Shadow Votes" = credit for being the first crewmate to correctly vote a specific impostor, but only while 7+ players are still active, and only one credit exists per impostor per game. Games logged before 8/18/2026 are excluded entirely from Shadow Vote tracking (both the count and the games it's measured against), since that logger update is what made vote order in the logs trustworthy.
